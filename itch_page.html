HOW IT WORKS

Set it once. Stay in the game.

GameBeats runs quietly in the background and reacts to what is actually happening on your PC — not a timer, not a guess.

01

It detects the game you launched.

Steam, Epic, or a custom executable. GameBeats notices the game, remembers the mood you mapped to it, and gets the right playlist ready.

02

It listens — passively.

WASAPI loopback audio monitoring compares game audio and Spotify output. When the game gets loud, music pauses. When the room goes quiet for a few seconds, music comes back.

03

It keeps the vibe going.

When a playlist ends, the puppy appears with a countdown and a “play next” prompt. Ignore it and the next playlist starts automatically. Click it, or use the global hotkey, without losing game focus.

FEATURES

Small app. Big quality-of-life energy.

Everything is built around one rule: the music should serve the game, not interrupt it.

◎

Automatic game detection

Recognizes running games from Steam, Epic, and custom executables, then switches to the playlist you mapped to that game.

◉

Audio-aware pause / resume

Listens to game and Spotify audio. Loud game audio pauses the music; quiet time brings it back after your chosen delay.

♪

Playlist moods

Map games to genres, moods, or curated Spotify playlists. Cozy game gets cozy music. Chaos game gets chaos music.

▣

Zero-focus-stealing puppy

The notifier uses native Windows flags so you can click it without losing keyboard or mouse focus in borderless and windowed games.

⌘

Global hotkey

Skip to the next playlist with a hotkey instead of alt-tabbing. Built for people who are mid-boss-fight.

⇄

Real Spotify shuffle sync

The new shuffle button talks directly to the Spotify web player, so the UI and the browser stay on the same page.

🐾

Desktop puppy toggle

Keep the little desktop puppy on for maximum companion energy, or switch it off from the footer when you want a cleaner screen.

◈

No desktop client required

GameBeats plays through its own browser-based Spotify session. Free or Premium account. No developer app setup.

UNDER THE HOOD

Cozy on the outside. Serious engineering underneath.

GameBeats is not a macro that clicks Spotify for you. It is a proper Windows music companion with its own detection and audio pipeline.

  • WASAPI loopback monitoring for game and music audio
  • Native Win32 no-activate notifier that stays out of your input path
  • Browser-based Spotify playback with local login persistence
  • Focus guard support for desktop Spotify clients
  • Local config and game mappings — no account data sent to us
